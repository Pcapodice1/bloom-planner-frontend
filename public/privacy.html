🌸 Privacy Policy

Bloom Planner · Last updated May 2026

Bloom Planner is a garden planning app that uses AI to generate personalized year-round bloom plans. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Do Not Collect

Bloom Planner does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not track your usage or location.

How the App Works

When you generate a garden plan, your preferences (hardiness zone, sun exposure, style, and color choices) are sent to our secure backend server, which forwards them to the Anthropic API to generate a plan. This data is not stored on our servers and is not associated with any personal identifier.

Plant photos are fetched from Unsplash using your plant search terms. No personal data is included in these requests.

Data Stored on Your Device

Garden plans you choose to save are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic API — used to generate garden plans. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash API — used to fetch plant photos. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Bloom Planner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
